The panel on the right is the real Chrome extension side panel. Here it runs with a fake chrome object and a mock backend, so nothing leaves this page. The mock "render" just pastes the garment onto your photo. The real API sends both images to an AI try-on model.

Try it in three steps

  1. Tick the boxes in the panel and press Agree and continue.
  2. Add a photo: or upload your own in the panel.
  3. Press Try on under the kurta below (or drag its image into the panel), then Try it on in the panel.
