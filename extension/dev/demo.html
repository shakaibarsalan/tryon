%BRAND_NAME%: see clothes on you before you buy

Pick a garment on any store, add one photo of yourself, and an AI try-on model shows you wearing it. The Try-On panel on this page is the real Chrome extension side panel, running with a stand-in for the browser APIs.

Real AI results

FASHN VTON 1.5 · about 30 s per render

Each result is the actual model output for the garment photo in its corner, made with the same request the API's Hugging Face provider sends (tools/sample-renders.mjs). The model and garment photos are AI-generated, so no real person's photo is used.

Try it yourself

  1. Tick the boxes in the panel and press Agree and continue.
  2. or upload your own photo in the panel.
  3. Press Try on under a garment below (or drag its picture into the panel), then Try it on in the panel.
