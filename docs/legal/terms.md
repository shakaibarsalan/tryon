Last updated: 25 November 2026

These terms cover the Try-On Chrome extension, the Try-On web page, the store script and the Try-On API. Try-On is a personal project by Shakaib Arsalan. By using it you agree to these terms and to the [privacy notice](privacy.md).

## What Try-On does

Try-On makes an AI picture of you wearing a garment you picked. The picture is a guess. Fit, length, colour, fabric and size can look different in real life, so don't treat it as sizing advice. The AI can also get things wrong: sleeves, prints, hands and faces are common trouble spots.

## Who can use it

You need to be 18 or older. Try-On isn't offered in Illinois, Texas or Washington State for now (see the privacy notice).

## Your photos

Only use photos of yourself. Don't upload or take photos of anyone else, of children, or nude or sexual photos. Don't use Try-On to make fake, humiliating or harmful pictures of anyone. We may block installs or networks that break this.

You keep every right to your photos and results. You give us permission to send your photo and the garment image to the render service for the single try-on you asked for, and for nothing else.

## Store images

Product photos belong to the stores and brands that publish them. Try-On reads the image you pick so it can make your personal preview, and doesn't keep it. Use your results for yourself. Don't publish them as if they were the store's own photos.

## Free try-ons and limits

Free try-ons are limited per install and per network each day, and there's a daily budget for everyone. We may change these limits, pause the service, or stop it.
